## Summary

**Voice Notes** (`/create/voice`) lets founders and product marketers record quick thoughts or upload audio clips, review the transcript, and convert them into structured **Publish** drafts.

When enabled for your workspace, **Voice notes** appears in the sidebar under **Create**.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Audio capture & upload | Record directly or upload audio files from the Shots app |
| Transcript review & redaction | Inspect generated transcripts and redact sensitive passages before generating drafts |
| Draft generation | Turn a voice note into one or more **Publish** drafts using your workspace brand voice |
| Audio lifecycle | Stream uploaded audio runs and delete source recordings when no longer needed |

## Workspace & Member Controls

Workspace admins enable or disable voice notes in **Settings → Voice notes** (`/settings/voice-notes`), where personal preferences and audio-upload defaults are also managed.
