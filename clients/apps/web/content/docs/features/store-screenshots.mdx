## Summary

**Store Screenshots** (`/create/store`) is Shots' dedicated studio for iOS App Store and Google Play listing visuals. It stays pinned under **Create** in the sidebar so product and growth teams can update store listings alongside every mobile release.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-screen sets | Arrange panoramic and side-by-side store slides in one synchronized canvas |
| Device mockups | Place mobile and tablet screenshots inside crisp device frames with matching padding and shadows |
| Typography & callouts | Pair headlines, subtitles, and badges with your workspace brand kit |
| Store-ready dimensions | Target App Store and Google Play aspect ratios without manual resizing |
| Saved projects | Every store set saves at `/create/store/<projectId>` and appears in **Library → Store screenshots** |
