## Summary

Shots enforces **human-only approval** on the server. AI workflows, Automate runs, workspace API keys, and MCP agents can draft campaigns, upload visuals, and submit posts for review—but **none of them can approve a post**.

## Post Approval Lifecycle

```text
DRAFT → READY_FOR_REVIEW → APPROVED → SCHEDULED → PUBLISHED
                         ↘ NEEDS_CHANGES / REJECTED
```

- Only signed-in workspace members whose role includes `release:approve` (and whose permission group does not deny it) can approve posts.
- A solo founder with the `owner` role can approve their own drafts; larger teams can separate `creator`, `approver`, and `publisher` responsibilities.
- Batch approval approves an entire launch atomically and writes a single audit record.
- External reviewers can inspect a read-only preview link at `/review/[token]`.

## Automated Guardrails

Before a post reaches approval or goes out, Shots checks:

- **Citation grounding:** Claims in the launch spec must cite the release brief or a numbered source; unsupported statements become open questions.
- **Copy checks:** Flags channel length overflows, banned hype terms from your brand kit, unknown URLs, and uncited claims.
- **Exact-version binding:** Editing a post or underlying dependency invalidates stale approval so revised materials are re-evaluated before delivery.
- **Send-time re-verification:** Durable Temporal workflows re-check approval state at send time and mark a post `PUBLISHED` only after receiving a network receipt. Uncertain deliveries move to `UNKNOWN_DELIVERY` for human review instead of retrying blindly.
