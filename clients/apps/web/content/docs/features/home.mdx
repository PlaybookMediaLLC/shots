## Summary

**Home** (`/home`) is the workspace entry point once you sign in. It surfaces recent activity, in-progress campaigns, content drafts, and quick shortcuts into **Create**, **Campaigns**, **Publish**, **Library**, and **Automate**.

Open it from **Home** at the top of the workspace sidebar or navigate directly to `/home`.

## Capabilities

| Area | What it provides |
| --- | --- |
| Quick creation | Jump directly into a new Screenshot, Canvas, Story Set, Store Screenshots project, Code image, or Publish draft |
| Recent work | Resume the visuals, drafts, and campaigns you edited most recently |
| Command search | Press `⌘K` anywhere in the shell to search destinations, drafts, projects, and Automate actions |
| Workspace switcher | Switch organizations, inspect your signed-in account, and open **Settings** from the sidebar footer |

## Prerequisites

Home requires a signed-in account in an active workspace on a paid plan. Workspaces without an active subscription see Pro indicators on locked areas and are guided to `/upgrade`.
