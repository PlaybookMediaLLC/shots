## Summary

**Publish → Analyze** (`/publish/analyze`) gives teams visibility into how published posts perform across connected social accounts without leaving Shots.

## Capabilities

| Section | Route | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | `/publish/analyze` | Delivery and engagement metrics scoped to the selected publishing profile and date range, with manual refresh controls |
| **Observations** | `/publish/analyze/observations` | Structured takeaways derived from recent post performance |
| **Best posting times** | `/publish/analyze/posting-times` | Recommended time slots per profile based on historical engagement and queue settings (also exposed via the `bestPostingTimes` API/MCP operation) |
| **Public analytics share** | `/analytics/public/[token]` | Read-only shareable analytics report for stakeholders or clients |
