## Summary

Shots separates **what your product is**, **how your brand looks and sounds**, and **where live screenshots come from** into dedicated settings pages under **Brand resources & content**.

## Sections

| Section | Route | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Settings → Brand voice & visual kits** | `/settings/brand-kit` | Tone rules, banned terms, copy constraints, primary colors, typography tokens, logos, and default editor layouts |
| **Settings → Product** | `/settings/product` | Product summary, target audience, positioning, and allowed claim boundaries used when drafting launch briefs |
| **Settings → Product pages** | `/settings/product-pages` | Registered live product URLs that campaigns screenshot, along with capture freshness tracking |
| **Settings → Shared links** | `/settings/shared-links` | Audit, copy, restrict, or revoke every design published at a public `/s/[token]` or embed link |

See [Brand Kits & Consistent Visuals](/docs/guides/brand-kit-and-visuals) for setup recommendations.
