## Summary

**Publish** (`/publish`) is Shots' social writing, scheduling, and delivery workspace. Posts are organized by **Profile** (also called a social set in the API), letting teams publish as founders, company handles, or client brands from one place.

## Sub-Views in Publish

| View | Route | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Posts** | `/publish`, `/publish/[draftId]` | Compose single posts or multi-part threads with rich media, mentions lookup, tags, pin/scratchpad properties, revision recovery, and threaded team comments |
| **Calendar** | `/publish/calendar` | Drag-and-drop editorial calendar with queue slots, scheduled posts, and internal calendar notes |
| **Bulk tools** | `/publish/batches`, `/publish/batches/[batchId]` | Import and manage batch drafts and bulk media posts |
| **Analyze** | `/publish/analyze` | Review post performance, observations, and best posting times — see [Publish Analytics](/docs/features/analytics) |
| **Link checks** | `/publish/link-checks` | Verify how URLs in your posts resolve as link cards (`og:title`, `og:image`, and broken-page warnings) before publishing |

## Durable Delivery Semantics

Scheduled posts run on Temporal workflows that survive server restarts and transient provider rate limits. Each delivery records per-account receipts and live links. If a social platform returns an ambiguous response, Shots marks the delivery for human verification rather than risking a duplicate post.
