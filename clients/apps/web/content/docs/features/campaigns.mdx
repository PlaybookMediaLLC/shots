## Summary

**Campaigns** (`/campaigns`) orchestrates the full path from a shipped release to an approved multi-channel launch. Every campaign moves through four explicit, reviewable stages so nothing goes out based on unverified claims.

## Campaign Stages

| Stage | Route | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Launch spec** | `/campaigns/[campaignId]/spec` | Shots reads the release notes, PRs, and attached sources to draft a structured launch brief. Every claim cites its source; unsupported claims surface as open questions for a human to confirm. |
| **Campaign plan** | `/campaigns/[campaignId]/plan` | Defines target channels (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Product Hunt, changelog, release email), messaging angles, visual capture hints, and a teaser / launch day / follow-up timeline. |
| **Assets** | `/campaigns/[campaignId]/assets` | Captures live product screens (or uses uploaded visuals), frames them in the Shots editor with your brand kit, critiques output quality, and stores editable layers. |
| **Copy & review** | `/campaigns/[campaignId]/copy` | Drafts channel-specific copy checked against character limits, banned hype words, unknown links, and citation integrity. Revisions arrive as diffs until applied. |

## Sub-Views

- **All campaigns (`/campaigns`):** Active and historical launch campaigns.
- **Release inbox (`/campaigns/releases`):** Incoming releases and merged pull requests waiting for qualification.
- **Deliveries (`/campaigns/deliveries`):** Per-channel delivery receipts and status across scheduled campaign posts.
- **Archived (`/campaigns?status=ARCHIVED`):** Completed or retired campaigns kept for historical reference.
