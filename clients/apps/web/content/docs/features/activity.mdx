## Summary

**Activity** (`/activity`) provides a chronological feed of workspace changes—campaign creations, approval decisions, scheduled posts, workflow runs, and draft updates—so everyone on the team can see what happened and who acted.

For compliance and security teams, Shots also maintains an append-only **Audit log** in **Settings → Audit log** (`/settings/audit-log`) with hashed IP addresses, 90-day default retention, legal hold, and signed SIEM log drains (Splunk, Datadog, or custom HTTPS endpoints) on Enterprise plans. See [Enterprise Security & Developer Controls](/docs/features/settings-enterprise).
