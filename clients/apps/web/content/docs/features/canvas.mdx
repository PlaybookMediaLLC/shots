## Summary

Beyond single screenshots, Shots provides two multi-element visual workspaces under **Create**:

- **Canvas (`/canvas`):** Compose multi-frame boards, layered diagrams, before/after comparisons, and annotated UI spreads on a flexible canvas.
- **Story Sets (`/creative-sets`):** Build coordinated multi-slide carousels and story packs (for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Product Hunt) that share one brand kit and consistent spacing.

## Capabilities

| Workspace | Route | Best used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Canvas** | `/canvas`, `/canvas/new`, `/canvas/[canvasId]` | Freeform product collages, multi-screen feature callouts, and spatial visual exploration |
| **Story Sets** | `/creative-sets`, `/creative-sets/[setId]` | Multi-slide release walkthroughs, LinkedIn document carousels, and vertical story packs |

## Organizing Saved Work

Every saved Canvas and Story Set is indexed in **Library** (`/library?type=canvas` and `/library?type=story`) so teammates can duplicate layouts, attach them to **Campaigns**, or publish them through **Publish**.
