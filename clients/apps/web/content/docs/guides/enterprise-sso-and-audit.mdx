Enterprise workspaces include dedicated identity, access-control, compliance, and branding controls under **Settings** (`/settings`).

## 1. Configure SAML 2.0 or OIDC SSO

Open **Settings → SSO** (`/settings/sso`):

1. Verify your organization's email domain(s).
2. Register your identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, or any standard SAML 2.0 / OIDC IdP).
3. Test sign-in with an admin account before enforcing SSO for all members.

For password-based accounts without SSO, members can enroll TOTP two-factor authentication under **Settings → Security** (`/settings/security`).

## 2. Enable SCIM 2.0 Provisioning

From **Settings → SSO**, generate a SCIM bearer token and configure your IdP to automatically provision, update, and deprovision workspace members as people join or leave your company.

## 3. Apply Permission Groups

Shots roles define baseline capabilities (`owner`, `admin`, `creator`, `approver`, `publisher`, `writer_publisher`, `viewer`). On Enterprise plans, **Settings → Permission groups** (`/settings/permission-groups`) lets admins create **subtractive** restriction groups that deny specific permissions beyond a member's base role—groups only subtract and never widen access.

## 4. Stream Audit Logs to Your SIEM

Open **Settings → Audit log** (`/settings/audit-log`):

- Browse and filter the append-only audit trail (with hashed IPs and 90-day default retention, extendable with legal hold).
- Configure signed HTTPS audit log drains to **Splunk**, **Datadog**, or any custom SIEM webhook endpoint.

## 5. Customize Workspace Branding

Open **Settings → White label** (`/settings/white-label`) to configure your organization's name, logo, favicon, and accent palette across the signed-in app.
