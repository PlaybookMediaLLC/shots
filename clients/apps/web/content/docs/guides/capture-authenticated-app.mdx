Many meaningful product updates live behind a login wall. Shots lets owners and admins configure **Signed-in capture** (`/workflows/connections`) so campaigns and workflows can screenshot authenticated screens automatically without manual uploads.

## Recommended Practice: Use a Dedicated Demo Account

Always create a dedicated, least-privilege demo or staging account populated with realistic synthetic data—never personal credentials or production customer data.

## Step 1: Create a Signed-In Connection

Open **Automate → Signed-in capture** (`/workflows/connections`) and add a connection for your application's base URL. Choose the sign-in method your app supports:

1. **Email & password:** Provide the login URL and encrypted demo account credentials. Shots signs in automatically inside an isolated Chromium session.
2. **Unattended email sign-in (magic link or OTP):** Provision a workspace-scoped `@capture` inbox address backed by Resend. Register that address as a demo user in your app. When a capture runs, Shots submits the email, waits for the verification link or OTP code to arrive, and completes sign-in automatically.
3. **Interactive session capture:** Sign in once through a guided session capture when your app uses a custom login flow; Shots monitors session health and alerts you if re-authentication is required.

## Step 2: Register Product Pages & Capture Goals

Open **Settings → Product pages** (`/settings/product-pages`) and add the routes you want Shots to keep fresh.

With **Zero-Config Autonomous AI Capture**, you do not need CSS selectors or brittle Playwright scripts:

- Describe the screen or UI state you want in plain language (for example, *"Open the Analytics tab, switch the range to 30 days, and expand the conversion chart"*).
- For multi-screen features, define a **capture sequence** so Shots steps through each view in order during one browser session.

## Step 3: Verify & Attach to Campaigns

Run a test capture directly from the connection or product page view to inspect the resulting screenshot and step trace. Verified captures save automatically to **Library → Assets** and are reused whenever a campaign or Automate workflow references that product page.
