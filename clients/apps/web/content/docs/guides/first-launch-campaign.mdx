This guide takes you from a completed product change to an approved, scheduled launch campaign.

## Prerequisites

- An active Shots workspace on Pro, Business, or Enterprise.
- Basic product context filled in under **Settings → Product**.
- At least one brand kit configured under **Settings → Brand voice & visual kits**.

## Step 1: Create or Qualify the Release

You can start a campaign in two ways:

- **From Release inbox (`/campaigns/releases`):** Select an incoming GitHub pull request or release event, verify the evidence and rollout scope, and click **Start campaign**.
- **Manually (`/campaigns/new`):** Paste your release notes, changelog bullet points, or PR links directly.

## Step 2: Review the Cited Launch Spec

Shots drafts a structured launch brief at `/campaigns/[campaignId]/spec`. Every factual claim links back to a numbered source from the release.

- Confirm or answer any **Open questions** where the source material did not state an answer explicitly.
- Edit any wording directly; Shots preserves version history so downstream deliverables stay bound to the verified facts.

## Step 3: Approve the Campaign Plan & Visuals

1. Open **Plan** (`/campaigns/[campaignId]/plan`) to choose channels (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Product Hunt, changelog, release email) and adjust the teaser, launch day, and follow-up cadence.
2. Open **Assets** (`/campaigns/[campaignId]/assets`) to inspect captured product screenshots and rendered editor cards. Click any visual to tweak framing, shadows, or overlays in the [Screenshot Editor](/docs/features/create).

## Step 4: Review Copy, Approve, and Schedule

Open **Copy** (`/campaigns/[campaignId]/copy`):

- Resolve any copy-check warnings (character limits, banned hype words, unknown links, or uncited claims).
- Submit drafts for review (`READY_FOR_REVIEW`).
- A signed-in teammate with `release:approve` permission approves individual posts or the entire launch batch in one click.
- Schedule approved posts to your connected profiles and track live receipts under **Campaigns → Deliveries** (`/campaigns/deliveries`).
