Use **Settings → Channels**, **Settings → Profiles**, and **Publish** to run a reliable multi-account publishing workflow with clear ownership and zero accidental sends.

## 1. Connect Social Channels

Open **Settings → Channels** (`/settings/channels`) and authorize the accounts your workspace posts from (such as X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Bluesky, or Mastodon).

## 2. Group Accounts Into Profiles

Open **Settings → Profiles** (`/settings/publishing-brands`) to organize channels into publishing identities (for example, a founder profile and a company profile):

- **General:** Profile name, visibility (shared profiles can be used by team members and scoped AI agents; private profiles stay restricted), and defaults.
- **Scheduling:** Configure recurring posting-slot queues and the profile time zone.
- **New draft:** Set default platforms and thread behavior for new drafts.
- **Writing Assistant:** Tune profile-specific drafting preferences.

## 3. Collaborate, Review, and Approve

1. Draft posts or threads in **Publish** (`/publish`) and attach visuals from **Create** or **Library**.
2. Use **Publish → Link checks** (`/publish/link-checks`) to confirm link previews render cleanly.
3. Exchange threaded feedback in draft comments before moving posts into review.
4. Ensure teammates who review hold the `approver`, `admin`, or `owner` role (which include `release:approve`). Only a signed-in person can approve; API keys and MCP agents can draft or submit for review, and `post:publish` remains off by default.

## 4. Monitor Deliveries & Posting Times

Track scheduled items on **Publish → Calendar** (`/publish/calendar`), check per-account delivery links in **Campaigns → Deliveries** (`/campaigns/deliveries`), and review engagement trends under **Publish → Analyze** (`/publish/analyze`).
