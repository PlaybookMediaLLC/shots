A well-configured brand kit ensures that manual visuals, Code Studio cards, and automated campaign assets all look and read like they came from the same team.

## 1. Define Brand Voice

Open **Settings → Brand voice & visual kits** (`/settings/brand-kit`):

- **Tone and audience guidance:** Describe how your team speaks (for example, concrete engineering details over vague marketing claims).
- **Banned hype terms:** Add words or phrases you never want in launch copy. Shots' copy checker automatically flags drafts that include them.
- **Formatting conventions:** Specify preferences around emoji usage, hashtag limits, and link placement.

## 2. Configure Visual Tokens & Logos

In the same section, upload your light and dark logo marks and define:

- Primary and surface colors
- Typography pairing and heading weights
- Default background style, corner radius, and shadow preferences

Shots automatically generates matching **Code Studio** syntax themes and **Screenshot Editor** presets from your brand kit colors.

## 3. Bind Brand Kits to Workflows

When editing an [Automate workflow](/docs/automate) or starting a new campaign, select the brand kit to apply. Every generated visual inherits those tokens while remaining fully editable in **Create** (`/editor`).
