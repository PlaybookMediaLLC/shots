Before inviting teammates or enabling automated workflows, configure the workspace foundations in **Settings** (`/settings`).

## Core Workspace Defaults

Open **Settings → General** (`/settings`) to configure:

- **Workspace name, slug, and logo** — identifies the workspace in the switcher, review links, and audit records.
- **Time zone** — anchors default posting queues, calendar views, and scheduled delivery times.
- **Release preferences** — sets default behavior for incoming releases and review expectations.

## Product Knowledge & Surfaces

Shots grounds launch copy and visuals in structured workspace context rather than guessing:

1. **Settings → Product** (`/settings/product`): define what your product does, who it serves, and what claims campaigns are allowed to make.
2. **Settings → Product pages** (`/settings/product-pages`): list the live product routes campaigns should capture, along with freshness status for each screen.
3. **Settings → Brand voice & visual kits** (`/settings/brand-kit`): configure voice guidelines, banned terms, colors, typography, and visual templates.

## Team Access & Roles

Open **Settings → Members** (`/settings/members`) to invite teammates and assign one of Shots' workspace roles (`owner`, `admin`, `creator`, `approver`, `publisher`, `writer_publisher`, or `viewer`).

On Enterprise workspaces, you can also configure **Settings → Permission groups** to subtract specific capabilities from selected members, **Settings → SSO** for SAML/OIDC and SCIM provisioning, and **Settings → Audit log** for SIEM drains. See [Roles & Permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Publishing Profiles & Integrations

- Connect social accounts in **Settings → Channels** (`/settings/channels`).
- Organize accounts into publishing identities in **Settings → Profiles** (`/settings/publishing-brands`), where each profile carries its own posting schedule, defaults, and writing assistant preferences.
- Connect GitHub repositories under **Automate → Repositories** (`/workflows/sources`) or set up API keys and webhooks under **Settings → Developer API** (`/settings/developers`).
