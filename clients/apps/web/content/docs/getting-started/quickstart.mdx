Follow this quickstart to go from a fresh Shots workspace to your first approved launch asset.

## 1. Create Your Workspace

Sign in at `/sign-in` using email/password, Google, GitHub, Microsoft, or your organization's SSO provider. Complete onboarding to create your workspace and select a plan (**Pro**, **Business**, or **Enterprise**).

During onboarding, Shots can inspect your public product URL to seed your product summary, target audience, and initial product pages. You can edit or replace every generated field later in **Settings → Product**.

## 2. Configure Your Brand Kit

Open **Settings → Brand voice & visual kits** (`/settings/brand-kit`):

- Set your **brand voice** rules, tone, and banned hype terms so AI-drafted copy matches how your team writes.
- Upload your logo and define primary colors, fonts, and visual defaults so screenshots and campaign cards stay consistent.

## 3. Create or Capture Visuals

You have two ways to produce visuals:

- **Manual studio (`/create`):** Open the [Screenshot Editor](/docs/features/create), [Store Screenshots](/docs/features/store-screenshots), [Canvas](/docs/features/canvas), or [Code Studio](/docs/features/code-studio) to style images and videos directly in the browser.
- **Automated capture (`/workflows/connections`):** Register your product pages in **Settings → Product pages** and optionally connect [Signed-In & AI Capture](/docs/features/signed-in-capture) so Shots can screenshot authenticated screens on every release.

## 4. Turn a Release Into a Campaign

Open **Campaigns** (`/campaigns`) and click **New campaign** (or start from a pull request in **Campaigns → Release inbox**):

1. Provide the release summary, PR links, or changelog notes.
2. Review the generated **Launch spec** — every claim cites its source, and uncited claims appear as open questions for a person to resolve.
3. Inspect the **Campaign plan**, generated visuals, and channel copy.
4. Approve the posts you want to ship and schedule them to your connected profiles in **Publish**.
